What MOC Source Can Do

Everything you need to source MOCs at the best price using Pick a Brick and BrickLink.

Sync Wanted Lists & Carts

Import your BrickLink wanted lists, BrickLink carts, and Pick a Brick cart with Save to MOC Source. Your parts stay synced locally so you can compare prices and continue where you left off.

Save Pick a Brick Carts

Save Pick a Brick carts for future purchases, Double Insider Points, and GWPs.

Import BrickLink Buy All

Import BrickLink wanted lists and Buy All carts directly into MOC Source.

Optimize Purchases

Automatically move parts to the cheapest source while keeping your carts synchronized.

Compare Prices

See Pick a Brick and BrickLink prices side by side while browsing.

Refresh Prices

Reload saved carts, refresh pricing, and re-optimize as Pick a Brick inventory and BrickLink prices change.

How it works

Three steps. No friction.

Install once, then shop on BrickLink the same way you always have.

1

Install the extension

Add MOC Source to Chrome or Brave from the Web Store. No account, no sign-in required.

2

Bring your wanted lists, carts, and PAB together

Open a BrickLink wanted list, BrickLink cart, or Pick a Brick cart, then click Save to MOC Source. Your parts are synced locally, allowing you to compare prices, optimize purchases, and pick up where you left off across sessions.

3

Buy at the best price

PAB prices appear inline on every list and cart page. Use Project Planning to split your order across PAB and BrickLink stores and see your total cost in real time.
